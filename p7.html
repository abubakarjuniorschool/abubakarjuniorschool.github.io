PRIMARY SEVEN

Preparing Learners For The Next Level

Primary Seven is an important stage in a learner's education. At Abubakar Junior School, we help our candidates develop the knowledge, confidence and discipline needed for their next level of education.

Our teachers work closely with learners to strengthen their understanding, examination skills, time management and personal responsibility.
