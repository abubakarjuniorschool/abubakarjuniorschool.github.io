ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Where Every Child Is Given A Chance To Shine

Abubakar Junior School is committed to providing quality education that develops the academic, social, moral and practical abilities of every learner.

We believe that education is more than classroom learning. Our goal is to nurture disciplined, confident and responsible children prepared for the future.
